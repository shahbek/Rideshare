# Landmarks — skyscrapers, churches, mosques, pagodas

Generated by `scripts/landmarks.py`. Landmarks use the footprint's **oriented bounding rectangle**
as a composition frame (`Frame`). `u` runs along the main axis from the entrance, `v` points left.
Every part is then placed in that frame: nave, tower, dome, minarets. Orientation comes from
`street` (a point) or `street_edge`. The entrance faces the street.

Reference renders: `assets/gallery_towers.png`, `assets/gallery_religious.png`.

## 1. Skyscrapers (`type: tower`)

**Composition:**
1. Repaired footprint (rounded if the preset has a radius).
2. **Setback tiers**: `setbacks = [(height_fraction, inset_m), ...]`. Each tier is the previous one
   buffered inward (mitre), re-rounded. A terrace roof is earcut between tiers, with AO near the
   next tier, plus a parapet sweep at every setback.
3. Optional **podium**: 3 storeys in stone with a cornice, tall windows and an arched door.
4. A **façade system** per tier.
5. A **crown**.

**Façade systems** (all near-zero unique triangles per floor):

| `facade` | Geometry | Reads as | Runtime alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| `grid` | dark glass walls + stone spandrel strips per floor (flat quads) + protruding piers every 2.6 m (`edge_box`) | Art Deco / Empire State vertical ribbing | piers stay geometry; spandrels → shader |
| `curtain` | glass walls + thin mullion sweep per floor (every 2nd floor above 40 floors) | modern glass | all → façade shader band pattern |
| `punched` | stone walls + instanced windows (1,000+ instances is fine) | classic 1910–30 towers | windows → façade shader at LOD1 |

**Crowns:**

| `crown` | Built from |
|---|---|
| `spire` | 3 stepped mini-setbacks + an 8-sided lathe mast (Empire State, Chrysler-lite) |
| `pyramid` | `ConvexRoof` at 56° (copper) + lantern drum + cone + gold finial (Metropolitan Life, Woolworth-lite) |
| `flat` | parapet + mechanical penthouse |
| `antenna` | flat + penthouse + thin lathe antenna (~22% of H) |

**Presets:**

| Preset | Setbacks | Façade | Crown |
|---|---|---|---|
| `art_deco` | 4 tiers | grid | spire |
| `modern_glass` | none, rounded | curtain | flat |
| `classic` | 1 + podium | punched | pyramid |
| `supertall` | 5 small, rounded | curtain | antenna |

Any preset key can be overridden in the spec (`setbacks`, `facade`, `crown`, `radius`, `floor_h`,
`wall`, `stone`). When OSM provides `building:part`s with heights, use their polygons directly as
tiers instead of buffered insets.

## 2. Churches (`type: church`)

| Style | Plan | Roof | Openings | Tower / domes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `gothic` | nave + transept (cruciform if L > 2.2 W) + apse | gable 55°, slate | lancet windows, **buttresses** between them, rose window | west tower: bands, belfry lancets, **clocks**, octagonal spire with corner pinnacles, cross |
| `romanesque` | same | gable 35°, terracotta | round-arched windows, oculus on the façade | **twin west towers** with steep pyramid roofs |
| `orthodox` | Greek cross (equal arms) + apse with half dome | gable 30°, blue | arched | central drum with arched windows + **gold onion dome**, 4 small blue onion domes at the re-entrant corners, crosses |
| `byzantine` | Greek cross + half-dome apse | gable 24°, tile | arched | drum + hemispherical dome with lip |

How the pieces work:
- **Plan:** a union of rectangles → `roof_for_footprint(kind="gable")` → `rect_cover` returns
  nave and transept as two wings. Equal pitches give exact valleys, and the gable infills become
  the façade and transept gables.
- **Apse:** a half-disc of walls plus a half cone (`lathe` with `a0..a1`) or a half dome
  (`dome(a0, a1)`).
- **Domes:** sit on `drum()` above the ridge. Small domes find their base with `RoofResult.z(p)`.

## 3. Mosques (`type: mosque`)

| Style | Hall & dome | Signature | Minarets |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ottoman` | square-ish hall (D ≈ 1.3 W), **shallow lead dome** on a 16-sided windowed drum, **two semi-domes** on the axis, **four weight turrets** with small domes | **courtyard (avlu)** with domed arcade portico on three sides and a central fountain (şadırvan) | slender "pencil": square base, 16-sided shaft, 2–3 balconies (şerefe), tall lead cone, alem finial |
| `persian` | tall drum, **pointed turquoise dome** | tall **iwan** portal (pointed-arch recess) on the entrance | two flanking, thicker, tile bands, balcony + bulb cap |
| `mughal` | raised **plinth**, **white onion dome** on a tall drum, **chhatris** (column kiosks) at the roof corners | iwan portal | four at the plinth corners, tapering, three balconies, chhatri tops |

Parameters: `minarets` (2 / 4 / 6), `courtyard` (bool), `minaret_height`.

Courtyard placement: if the footprint is long enough (L − D ≥ 0.6 W), the first part of it becomes
the courtyard. Otherwise the courtyard is added in front of the entrance only when requested
explicitly.

Ottoman minaret layouts follow the classic patterns:
- 2 minarets at the hall's entrance corners.
- 4 minarets: the hall entrance corners plus the far courtyard corners (or all 4 hall corners if
  there's no courtyard).
- 6 minarets: hall corners plus far courtyard corners.

## 4. Pagodas (`type: pagoda`)

Square tiers shrinking 13% per level, each with a band and a `curved_hip` roof (concave profile,
upturned eave, 26% overhang). A bronze sōrin finial (stacked lathe rings) sits on top.
`style: japanese` (dark wood) or `chinese` (vermilion). `tiers` defaults to 5.

## 5. Budgets and LOD

| Landmark | Unique shell (LOD0) | Instances |
|---|---|---|
| Art Deco tower, 300 m | ~5k | ~230 |
| Glass tower, 200 m | ~5k (bands) | 0 |
| Classic tower, 150 m | ~0.7k | ~1,700 windows |
| Gothic church | ~0.8k | ~45 |
| Orthodox church | ~1.8k | ~27 |
| Ottoman mosque + courtyard | ~3.7k | ~165 |
| Mughal mosque | ~5k | ~105 |
| Pagoda | ~0.5k | 0 |

Landmarks are few per tile, so they may exceed the ordinary-building budget. Keep them under ~8k
unique triangles.

**LOD1:**
- Drop instances. The façade shader takes the windows.
- Reduce dome and lathe sections by half (`sections` parameter).
- Drop minaret balconies to one.
- Skip piers and mullions.

**LOD2:** a prism with the roof form only. Keep the dome and spire silhouettes (they're the
landmark's identity at distance) as low-section lathes: 8 sections, 4 profile points.

## 6. Matching a real landmark

For named buildings that people look for (the Empire State Building, Hagia Sophia, the Blue
Mosque, Notre-Dame), start from the closest generator and override parameters:
- tier heights and insets from `building:part` data,
- dome radius and drum height,
- minaret count and height,
- colours.

If that still doesn't read, author a mid-poly library mesh (SKILL.md budgets, one bevel segment,
smooth normals) and place it as a single instance.

Never decimate a photogrammetry mesh for this style: the result looks melted, not stylized.
